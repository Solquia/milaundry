# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Laundry shop owners, staff, and customers in the Philippines. The `/download` page is for anyone given the link who needs the Android preview on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Milaundry is the operating system for a neighbourhood laundry: booking, the till, weighing, payment, receipts, and order tracking. Success is a shop running its day in the app, and a customer following a load without a phone call.

## Positioning

The shop is the product. Customers meet a laundry's own page, not a marketplace of shops. QR codes at the counter connect a walk-in ticket to a phone.

## Operating Context

Opened on cheap Android phones at a wet counter, and in a browser from a QR code or a texted link. Preview builds are sideloaded as an APK, not listed on Play Store yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Android preview APK at `/download`.
- iPhone cannot install that APK; the page offers the web app instead.
- Someone already inside the native app is not asked to download it.
- No Play Store or App Store listing. Do not claim one.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Milaundry** (no "3.0" in the display name).
- Washer-face app icon at `assets/images/icon.png`.
- Voice: short, concrete, counter-side. The copy says what to do, not what the product is.

## Evidence on Hand

- App icon: `assets/images/icon.png`
- Current preview APK URL lives in `src/lib/domain/download.ts` (`APK_URL`).
- No testimonials, ratings, or install counts. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- One job per screen.
- Name the action, not the category.
- Do not claim a store listing, a price, or a customer the product does not have.
- Sideload instructions stay honest about what Android will ask.
